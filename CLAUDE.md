## Audience: mostly elderly visitors

This site is used mainly by older people. Design and copy for that, always:

- Body text at least 16px (prefer 17–18px); nothing meaningful below 14px, and no tiny uppercase/letter-spaced labels for important information.
- Strong contrast (WCAG AA minimum, aim higher). No light-grey-on-white or semi-transparent text for anything that matters.
- Large tap targets (at least 44×44px) with clear visible focus states; never rely on small icon-only buttons without generous padding and a text label where possible.
- Plain, short wording. Put key facts (what, when, where) up front. Avoid jargon and clever UI.
- Keep navigation simple and predictable. Avoid auto-appearing popups, carousels, timed content and anything that moves; where a dialog is unavoidable it must be easy to dismiss with a large, labelled button.
- Check every change at phone width as well as desktop, and keep full keyboard and screen-reader support.
